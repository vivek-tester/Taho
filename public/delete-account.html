Taho — Account & Data Deletion

In compliance with the Google Play User Data & Account Deletion Policy, this web resource allows Taho users to request permanent deletion of their account and associated server data without needing to reinstall the application.

Warning: Permanent Deletion
Deleting your account will permanently delete your authentication record, cloud user profile, and subscription associations from our Supabase backend. This action cannot be reversed.

Method 1: In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you still have the Taho app installed on your Android device, you can delete your account instantly:

  1. Open Taho on your device.
  2. Tap Settings (gear icon) → tap your Profile.
  3. Scroll to ACCOUNT ACTIONS → tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm deletion. Your account and cloud data will be wiped immediately.

What Happens After Submission

  • Verification: A verification confirmation will be sent to the submitted email to confirm ownership.
  • Data Purged: Authentication credentials, email address, profile names, and cloud session records are purged within 48 hours of verification (in-app deletion is instantaneous).
  • Local Data: Local API requests, history, and collections on previously used devices remain local to the device and can be wiped via Android Application Settings → Clear Storage.
  • Financial Retention: Financial transaction identifiers required under applicable statutory laws (such as GST records) may be retained in anonymized format solely for tax compliance.
